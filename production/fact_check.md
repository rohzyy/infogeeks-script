# Fact-check report — H-1B Visa documentary

Prepared 14 October 2026. Covers every legally consequential, numerical and time-sensitive claim in `H1B_Visa.pdf`, checked against the leads in `h1b_editor_links.md` and further searches.

## Method and limits (read first)

- **How sources were checked:** this production environment's network policy blocks every news, law-firm and government host (pexels.com, pixabay.com, uscis.gov, dol.gov, theprint.in, etc. all return `connect_rejected`), and the page-fetch tool could not resolve those hosts either. The only research channel that worked was a **web search tool that returns result titles, URLs and summarised snippets**.
- **So, honestly:** no original article, court record or government page was opened and read in full during this session. "Corroborated" below means *multiple independent search results, with titles and summaries from named outlets, agree*. That is weaker than reading the original, and the brief rightly says a snippet is not equivalent to verifying the source. Every item marked **Corroborated (snippets)** should be re-read at source before publication; the planned screenshot of each source doubles as that check.
- **Status scale:** Verified-official (primary government source located) · Corroborated (multiple outlets agree, originals not opened) · Reported (one credible outlet) · Official claim (stated by an official; not independently tested) · Projection · Opinion · Unverified · Corrected (script wording changed).
